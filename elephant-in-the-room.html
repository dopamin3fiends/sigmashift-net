HOW IT WORKS
FREE

Solo Mode

Describe your side of a personal dilemma, workplace conflict, or family disagreement. Upload evidence if you have it. The AI reviews everything and gives you a structured, honest analysis — what's actually going on, what the evidence supports, and practical next steps.

FROM $2.99

Multi-Party Mediation

For disputes between two people. Both sides submit their perspective and evidence privately — neither party sees what the other wrote. The AI asks each side targeted follow-up questions, then delivers a final objective verdict: a summary, an evidence review, and a timeline of events.

WHAT YOU GET
  • — An objective written analysis of your dispute, grounded in what you actually submitted
  • — Evidence review — the AI reads and references your uploaded documents directly
  • — A visual timeline mapping out how the situation unfolded
  • — Blind cross-examination that surfaces contradictions and gaps neither side may have noticed
  • — Practical, actionable next steps — not just "who's right"
WHAT THIS ISN'T

Elephant in the Room is an AI analysis tool, not a lawyer, therapist, or financial advisor. It doesn't provide legal, medical, or financial advice, and using it doesn't create any kind of professional relationship. It's built to help you think more clearly about a conflict — not to replace a licensed professional when you need one.
