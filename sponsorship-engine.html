// THE PROBLEM

Sponsor lists are built by hand.

Every new city or event date means weeks of searching maps, copying contact details into spreadsheets, guessing which businesses are a fit, and sending emails one at a time. Most of that work is repeatable. SponsorshipEngine does it as one pipeline, so your team spends its time on the conversations that close deals.

// HOW IT WORKS

One pipeline, five stages.

01 / DISCOVER

Map the market

Grid-based regional search on Google Maps data finds every relevant business in a city or region, with no duplicates.

02 / ENRICH

Find the contact

Websites are crawled for contact details and each business is categorized by industry.

03 / VERIFY

Check before you send

Email addresses are verified and bad leads are filtered out, which protects your sender reputation.

04 / SCORE

Rank by fit

Leads are cleaned and scored, so the best-fit sponsors are contacted first.

05 / REACH OUT

Paced outreach

Branded pitch emails go out from your own mailbox, at a safe pace, and every send is logged.

// BUILT FOR REAL CAMPAIGNS

Guardrails included.

  • — Mission Control desktop app: the whole pipeline in one Windows program, no terminal needed.
  • — One-button pipeline: checkpoints at every stage, and it resumes automatically after a failure.
  • — Separate campaigns: each campaign keeps its own leads, logs, limits, and results.
  • — Spend caps: a dry-run cost estimate and a hard monthly cap on map-data spend.
  • — Your brand, your mailbox: each client has its own profile with sender, templates, and assets.
  • — Engineered to last: an automated test suite, CI, dependency audits, and a documented threat model.
